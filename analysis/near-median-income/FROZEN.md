# Near-Median Income Share — FROZEN methodology and endpoint series

Status: FROZEN for the American Economic Bargain project, September 2026.

## Canonical methodology
- Universe: U.S. household population; exclude group quarters.
- Household-size adjustment: household money income / sqrt(household size).
- Reference median: household-weighted median of adjusted household income.
- Outcome unit: people. Each person inherits the adjusted income of their household.
- Primary belt: Near-Median Core, 90%–110% of the reference median.
- Sensitivity belts: 80%–120%; two-thirds to twice median.
- Always report below / within / above shares.
- Absolute headcount is secondary only.
- Fixed DFA middle 40% remains a separate wealth metric and is not interpreted as the size of the middle class.

## Interpretation
A decline in a middle belt means the distribution is less concentrated around that median-defined range. It does not by itself imply deterioration or individual downward mobility. Direction must be assessed from the below and above shares. Repeated cross-sections do not track the same individuals over time.
